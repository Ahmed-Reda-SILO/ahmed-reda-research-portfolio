## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am an interdisciplinary researcher and academic working at the intersection of **low-power analog/mixed-signal integrated circuits, biomedical sensor interfaces, electronic design automation, embedded systems, IoT, energy harvesting, and connected sensing technologies**.

I received my Ph.D. in **Electronic Science and Technology** from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University**, China, in 2021, where I conducted research at the **Ministry of Education Key Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence**. I am currently a **Postdoctoral Fellow** with the **Interdisciplinary Research Center for Communication Systems and Sensing (IRC-CSS), King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM)**, Saudi Arabia.

My research spans the complete sensing chain—from **transistor-level CMOS circuits and analog front ends** to **sensor acquisition, firmware, hardware/software co-design, wireless connectivity, signal processing, and application-level validation**.

My current work focuses on three complementary research directions:

- **NexusAFE** — configurable ultra-low-power multi-sensor analog interfaces
- **HEMOX** — multichannel wearable hemodynamic monitoring
- **SILO** — variability-aware analog/RFIC optimization and verification

## 🚀 Funded & Featured Research Projects

### 📡 IN26115 · NexusAFE — Configurable Ultra-Low-Power Multi-Sensor Analog Interface Platform

**Project No. IN26115 · 2026–2029**  
**Research Team Member / Co-PI · KFUPM Deanship of Research**  
**Total Project Funding: SAR 356,500**

Development of a configurable **CMOS/COTS analog front-end platform** for multi-sensor IoT nodes. NexusAFE integrates **PVT-robust analog circuits, time-based conversion, synchronized acquisition, wireless sensing, and application-level validation** to bridge physical and biomedical sensing with connected intelligence.

---

### 🫀 INCS2606 · HEMOX — Multichannel Hemodynamic Monitoring Platform

**Project No. INCS2606 · 2026–2027**  
**Research Team Member / Co-PI · IRC-CSS**  
**Total Project Funding: SAR 50,000**

Development of a synchronized **dual-site, dual-wavelength PPG sensing platform** for wearable hemodynamic monitoring. HEMOX integrates **low-power wireless acquisition, motion- and signal-quality-aware processing, physiological-parameter estimation, and real-time analysis through HEMOX Explorer**.

---

### ⚡ INCS2501 · Integrated RF–DC Rectifier for Energy Harvesting

**Project No. INCS2501 · 2025–2026**  
**Research Team Member / Co-PI · IRC-CSS**  
**Total Project Funding: SAR 63,300**

Development of high-efficiency **CMOS RF–DC rectifier architectures** for low-power IoT and energy-harvesting applications, including **dual dynamic self-biasing, three-phase conversion, and generalized multiphase multistage folded cross-coupled rectifier architectures**.

---

### 🤖 SILO — Simulator-in-the-Loop Analog/RFIC Design

**SILO — Spectre-Driven Simulator-in-the-Loop Framework for Variability-Aware Analog/RFIC Optimization and Verification**

An automated **Cadence Spectre-integrated design framework** combining circuit optimization, **PVT-corner analysis, Monte Carlo variability assessment, verification, and robustness evaluation** to improve analog/RFIC design reproducibility and design-to-signoff efficiency.
